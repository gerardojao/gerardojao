About me

I am Gerardo and I am

I am a Full-Stack Developer focused on building reliable software that solves real business problems. My professional experience spans frontend development with React and JavaScript, backend development with .NET and C#, REST APIs, SQL Server, and desktop applications built with WPF and the MVVM architecture pattern.


I work across the complete product lifecycle: understanding operational needs, designing and implementing features, improving existing systems, and delivering maintainable solutions. My background in accounting also gives me a practical perspective when translating financial and administrative processes into software.


I am currently building ZagaPro, a productivity SaaS designed to help automotive workshops centralize their daily operations. It brings together customers, vehicles, estimates, work orders, inventory, purchases, delivery notes, and invoicing in one platform, turning complex workflows into a clear and efficient digital experience.


I am committed to continuous learning, clean code, and creating products that provide measurable value to the people and businesses that use them.
